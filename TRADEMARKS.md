page explains how you may use them. It is written in plain English, in the spirit of the
trademark policies of other open-source projects.

## What you may do without asking

- **Refer to the project**: write about OpenRows, review it, teach it, or say that you use it.
- **Say your product works with it**: "compatible with OpenRows", "an integration for OpenRows",
  "runs on OpenRows", as long as nobody could think your product is made or endorsed by us.
- **Link to us** with the unmodified name or logo: to [openrows.io](https://openrows.io), the
  hosted service or this repository.
- **Self-host it**: run an unmodified or modified copy for your own organization's internal use,
  with the name and logo as they ship.
- **Redistribute unmodified copies** of an official release, under its own name.

## What needs our permission

- **Modified versions you distribute or offer to others** (a fork, a rebranded build, a hosted
  service for other organizations): don't call them OpenRows or use the rose logo as their name or
  logo, and remove the OpenRows branding (the name in the interface, the logo, icons, emails and
  sign-in theme). You may say truthfully that your product is "based on OpenRows".
- Using the name or logo in your product, company or domain name, or in a way that suggests we
  sponsor, endorse or certify you.
- Changing the logo, or making merchandise with it.
